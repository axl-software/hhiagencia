# HHA Brand Foundation V1.1

## Naming
- Tentative legal name: **HHA Digital Solutions SpA**
- Mother brand: **HHA Digital Solutions**
- Short forms: **HHA Digital / HHiAgencia**
- Preferred commercial name: **HHiAgencia**
- Ideal trademark: **HHA** or **HHA Digital Solutions**

## Website naming
- Visible website name: **HHiAgencia**
- SEO title: **HHA Digital Solutions | Web, Automatización y Marketing**
- Footer commercial/legal name: **HHA Digital Solutions**

## Positioning
**Ayudamos a marcas, creadores y empresas con contenido, estrategias, automatizaciones y soluciones digitales.**

## Primary hook
**Digitaliza. Automatiza. Escala.**

## Personality
Modern, premium, minimalist, technological, corporate, close, human, clear and practical.

## Tone
Neutral Spanish, direct, clear, educational, approachable and professional.

Use “tú” by default; “usted” when context requires it.

## Visual direction
Modern, premium, minimal, tech-oriented, dark + light, clean and structured.

Avoid generic AI gradients, random neon overload, robots/brains/circuit clichés, excessive glassmorphism and visual noise.

## Logo
Current logo is preserved. Do not redesign or replace it without explicit approval.
